###### **1 4 · F A Q** 

## **The honest answers.** 

If your question isn't here, ask us directly. We don't hide behind FAQ pages — we have conversations. 

#### **General** 

#### **What is VRYKS?** 

VRYKS is a full-stack creative and media studio — strategy, design, technology, marketing, and production under one roof. Not an agency. A studio that operates as an integrated extension of your brand. 

#### **Where is VRYKS based?** 

India. Operating globally. We work with clients across time zones — the work doesn't stop when the sun sets in one country. 

#### **Who are the founders?** 

Sagnik Dutta (Narrative & Strategy), Ishan Mitra (Vision & Form), and Debayan Mukherjee (Stack & Framework). Three specialists. One studio. Every discipline covered. 

#### **Working With Us** 

#### **How do I start working with VRYKS?** 

Book a 45-minute working session. We come prepared with research on your brand, competitors, and digital presence. No pitch, no pressure. If we're the right fit, a proposal follows within 24–48 hours. 

#### **What is the minimum budget to work with VRYKS?** 

We don't publish pricing because every engagement is scoped to the specific outcome. VRYKS is not the cheapest option — and that is the point. We compete on outcome, not price. 

#### **How long does a typical project take?** 

Scope-dependent. Brand identity system: 4–8 weeks. Full website: 6–12 weeks. Campaign: 3–6 weeks. Your creative lead provides exact timelines during the proposal phase. 

#### **Do you work with startups or only established brands?** 

Both. What matters is not the size of your business — it's the size of your ambition. If you're serious about building a brand that compounds, we're serious about partnering with you. 

#### **Can I hire VRYKS for a single service (e.g., just a logo)?** 

We can, but we'll always contextualise it within a larger strategy. A logo without positioning is just a drawing. If that's all you need, we'll tell you honestly — and if there's more to build, we'll show you why. 

#### **Process** 

#### **Will I deal with a project manager or the actual creative team?** 

The builder. Your creative lead is the same person designing your brand, writing your copy, or building your website. No relays. No coordinators. The person who built it is the person who answers your call. 

#### **How does feedback work?** 

Feedback at VRYKS is a creative conversation. The work you receive is not a draft — it's a considered position. We ask that feedback be specific, outcome-referenced, and consolidated in one round per deliverable cycle. 

#### **How many revisions are included?** 

Defined in your project agreement — typically 2–3 rounds per deliverable. Requests beyond scope are flagged, quoted, and approved in writing before any additional work begins. Transparent. Upfront. No surprises. 

#### **What happens after the project is delivered?** 

We don't hand off and disappear. Post-delivery support includes bug fixes (30 days), content updates (14 days), and strategic consultation (anytime). For Membership and Residency clients, support continues uninterrupted. 

#### **Pricing & Payments** 

#### **What are your engagement models?** 

Three: Creative Breakthrough (project-based), Studio Membership (monthly), and Creative Residency (embedded team). Each is designed around different needs — we'll recommend the right model during your working session. 

#### **What are your payment terms?** 

Typically 50% advance, 25% at mid-milestone, 25% on delivery. All terms are defined in your confirmation invoice before work begins. 

#### **Do you offer refunds?** 

Cancellation within 72 hours of signing: full refund. After work commences: the advance is non-refundable, and completed work is invoiced. 

#### **Technical** 

#### **What tech stack do you use?** 

Depends on the project. We work across WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, custom React/Next.js, AWS, Vercel, HubSpot, Zoho, and more. The stack serves the outcome, not the other way around. 

#### **Do you provide hosting?** 

Yes. Managed hosting on AWS, Vercel, or custom configurations — auto-renewed, monitored, and maintained. Your infrastructure runs without you thinking about it. 

#### **Do I own the final files?** 

All IP transfers to you upon full payment. Source files, brand assets, code repositories — everything. We retain copies for continuity of service, available upon request.
